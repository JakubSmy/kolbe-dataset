# 0001. One company on both sides of Meridian's books

Status: accepted.

## Context

The brief allows a Supplier, a Customer or both. In real books one relationship often causes problems on both sides, and the two sides touch each other.

## Decision

Kolbe Industrietechnik GmbH is both. Meridian buys hardware from Kolbe under framework agreement KOL-MDS-FWA-2025-001 (German law). Kolbe changed its bank details by letter in March 2026, as FWA §7.2 allows; there is no amendment. Kolbe uses Meridian's platform under MDS-KOL-SUB-2025-001 (Spanish law), EUR 5,000.00 a month.

## Consequences

- Both agreements have a set-off rule: only with written agreement of both parties for each set-off (FWA §8, SUB §6). The decoy s6 uses it: in June both sides agreed in writing to set RE-2026-0270 off against MDS-2026-06.
- The same rule makes one step wrong in p2: Meridian may deduct the issued credit note (Kolbe allows it in writing, mail 67), but not the disputed EUR 210.00 before Kolbe agrees.
- Each agreement has its own bank-change rule (FWA §7.2 for Kolbe's bank, SUB §5.2 for Meridian's). The cash discount (SUB §4.5) exists only on Meridian's side.
- Both sides invoice cross-border, so no invoice carries VAT.
