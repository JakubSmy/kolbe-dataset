## 2. Subject

2.1 The Provider gives the Customer access to the Meridian data platform (the "Platform"). The Customer uses it to collect, store and analyse telemetry from its warehouse and from its device fleet of edge gateways and sensors.

2.2 Named users. The Customer uses the Business plan, which covers up to 250 named users. A named user is one person with a personal login. Named users must not share their logins. Each named user above the limit costs EUR 25.00 per month. The Provider counts the named users on the first day of each month. Users above the limit are billed monthly.

2.3 Support. The fee includes 20 hours of expert support per calendar quarter. Time spent on faults of the Platform is not counted. Further hours cost EUR 120.00 per hour.

## 3. Fee

3.1 The Customer pays EUR 5,000.00 per month for the Business plan, without VAT.

3.2 The service is a business-to-business service to a business established in Germany. The place of supply is Germany (Article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC). The Customer pays the German VAT under the reverse charge (Article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices show no VAT, the VAT IDs of both parties and the note "Reverse charge".

3.3 The fee in Section 3.1 is fixed for the initial term in Section 7.1.

## 4. Invoicing and payment

4.1 The Provider issues one invoice on the 1st day of each month, in advance for that month, in EUR. It shows the fee of Section 3.1 and any named users above the limit under Section 2.2 and support hours due under Section 2.3.

4.2 Each invoice is payable within 30 days of its issue date (Net 30). If a due date is not a business day in Barcelona, payment on the next business day is on time.

4.3 The Customer pays the invoiced amount without any deduction, except a cash discount under Section 4.5 or a set-off agreed under Section 6.

4.4 Amounts not paid by the due date bear late payment interest under Spanish Law 3/2004 of 29 December, at the European Central Bank rate plus 8 percentage points. The Provider may also claim the fixed compensation of EUR 40.00 under Article 8 of Law 3/2004.

4.5 The Customer may deduct a 2% cash discount if payment is received within 10 days of the invoice date.

6.1 Amounts under this agreement may be set off against amounts under the Framework Supply Agreement KOL-MDS-FWA-2025-001 between the same parties only with the written agreement of both parties for each set-off. Email is enough.

6.2 The written agreement names the invoices and the amounts set off.

6.3 Sections 6.1 and 6.2 also apply to any set-off between amounts under this agreement.

## 7. Term

7.1 This agreement runs from 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2027 (the initial term).

7.2 It renews for further periods of 12 months unless either party gives written notice at least 3 months before the end of the current term.

7.3 After the initial term, the Provider may change the fee for a renewal period by written notice at least 3 months before that period starts.

## 8. Governing law

8.2 The courts of Barcelona have exclusive jurisdiction.

8.3 Changes to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.
